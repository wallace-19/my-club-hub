Privacy Policy

Last updated: January 2026. We take your privacy seriously. This policy explains what data we collect, how we use it, and how we protect it.

1. Information we collect

When you use ClubHub, we collect the following types of information:

  • Account information — your name, email address, and password when you register.
  • Club data — member records, attendance logs, event details, and payment records that you or your club administrators enter into the system.
  • Usage data — how you interact with the platform, including pages visited and features used, to help us improve the product.
  • Device information — browser type, IP address, and device type for security and performance purposes.

2. How we use your information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Provide and maintain the ClubHub platform and its features.
  • Send you important account and service notifications.
  • Improve, personalise, and expand our services.
  • Monitor usage for security, fraud prevention, and platform integrity.
  • Respond to your support requests and communications.

We do not sell your personal data to third parties. We do not use your data for advertising.

3. Data storage and security

All data is encrypted in transit using TLS and encrypted at rest. We implement role-based access controls so that only authorised users can access specific data. Admins have full audit logs of all actions taken within their club.

We retain your data for as long as your account is active. If you delete your account, your personal data is removed within 30 days, except where we are required to retain it by law.

4. Data sharing

We only share your data with third parties in the following circumstances:

  • Service providers — trusted providers who help us operate the platform, bound by confidentiality agreements.
  • Legal requirements — if required by law, regulation, or a valid legal process.
  • With your consent — for any other purpose, only with your explicit consent.

5. Your rights

You have the right to access, correct, delete, or export your personal data. To exercise any of these rights, contact us at privacy@clubhub.co.ke.

6. Cookies

ClubHub uses session cookies to keep you logged in. We do not use tracking or advertising cookies.

7. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time and will notify you of significant changes via email or in-app announcement.

8. Contact us

Questions about this policy? Contact us at privacy@clubhub.co.ke.
